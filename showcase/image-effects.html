🔥 Image Effects Module 2.0

Cinematic Image FX & Hover Suite

A premium collection of high-performance hover, reveal, and viewport-entrance effects. Custom built for Awwwards-level interactive graphics and layouts.

Shine Sweep

Classic diagonal gleam sweep across the image wrapper. Ideal for premium product listings and button surfaces.

Circle Ripple

A light circular highlight zooms out from the point of mouse center and fades out, creating a water ripple effect.

Blur → Sharp

Soft blur focus transitions to a sharp image structure. Simulates cinematic lens focusing on intersection or hover.

3D Interactive Tilt

Calculates mouse coordinates to dynamically tilt the image block in 3D perspective with realistic shadow offsets.

Chromatic RGB Split

Deconstructs the image into red and blue channel slices shifting on active mouse states to create chromatic aberration.

Slide Overlay

A solid color block panel slides smoothly over the image. Direction configurable to Up, Down, Left, or Right.

Liquid Warp

Interactive canvas displacement map creating fluid ripples and wave movement responding to hover triggers.

Retro Pixelate

Starts with a low-res pixelated filter structure and resolves to a crystal-clear original rendering.
